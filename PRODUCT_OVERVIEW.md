# Signus - Couple Traffic Light

## A Support Tool for Couples Therapy and Healthy Relationships

## Ideal Scenario in Couples Therapy

In therapy, the goal is for couples to achieve **open communication**, especially around intimate topics. Clear and honest verbal communication is essential to understand each partner's needs, desires, and boundaries.

However, **at the beginning of therapy**, when this level of openness has not yet been established, or when emotional and psychological barriers are present (such as embarrassment or insecurity when expressing sexual desire), **verbal communication can become difficult**.

This is where ***Signus – Couple Traffic Light*** can help **facilitate that initial step**.

---

## Another Use Case: Healthy Relationships Without Therapy

Even in **healthy relationships**, couples may not always be aware of when their partner feels available or receptive to intimacy. This lack of awareness can lead to misunderstandings, frustration, or discomfort.

In these situations, the app acts as a **preventive support tool**, helping reduce friction between partners. By providing a simple and discreet way to express availability, couples can **stay better aligned** and avoid situations where one partner feels unsure about how to approach the other.

### Additional Benefits

- **Reduced misunderstandings:**  
  Helps avoid misinterpreting silence or unclear signals.

- **Healthier communication patterns:**  
  Encourages respectful and more transparent expression of needs.

- **Conflict prevention:**  
  Reduces moments of tension caused by mismatched expectations.  
  It does not replace conversation, but **makes it easier to initiate**.

---

## How the App Helps

1. **Encouraging gradual openness**  
   The app allows couples to begin expressing their needs without the immediate pressure of verbalizing them.  
   This creates space for **deeper conversations later on**.

2. **Reducing discomfort**  
   Discussing intimacy can be emotionally difficult.  
   The traffic light provides a **gentle entry point**, lowering the barrier to communication.

3. **Supporting emotional alignment**  
   By sharing availability, couples can better **synchronize expectations**, reducing misunderstandings.

4. **A complement to therapy, not a substitute**  
   The app does not replace necessary conversations.  
   It **supports non-verbal expression** and can help make therapy more effective over time.

5. **Useful in both early therapy and stable relationships**  
   It is particularly valuable in early therapy stages, but also in healthy relationships where couples want to avoid unnecessary friction.

## Therapeutic Context

***Signus – Couple Traffic Light*** is not intended to replace communication or eliminate the need for conversation.

Instead, it helps **ease the transition toward more open and effective communication**, particularly in sensitive contexts.

It can:

- act as a **bridge** toward more open dialogue
- help couples **reduce emotional resistance**
- support **progressive communication development**

Over time, as communication improves, the need for the app may naturally decrease.
